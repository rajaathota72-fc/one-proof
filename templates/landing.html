Pipeline

How it works

Three steps, fully automated, end to end.

1

Face detection & encoding

Detects the face in your photo and encodes it into a 128-dimension vector, then hashes it (SHA-256) — the raw biometric never leaves your machine.

2

Real web / social search

Runs a genuine reverse-image search (Google Lens via SerpApi) against the live web to find an actual matching post — not a hardcoded result.

3

Soulbound badge minted on-chain

Hashes the matched post and mints a non-transferable NFT to your wallet on Sepolia, then re-reads it on-chain to prove the record hasn't been tampered with.
