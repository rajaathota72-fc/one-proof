Use cases

Where "Proof of Human" matters

Any place fake profiles and bots cause real harm.

01

Dating apps

Stop catfishing before the first message. A verified badge proves the profile photo belongs to a real, findable person — not a stolen or AI-generated image.

02

DAOs & governance

Sybil-resist voting and airdrops. One soulbound badge per wallet means one real human, one voice — no bot farms gaming quorum.

03

Freelance marketplaces

Clients hire real people with a real online track record, not throwaway accounts. The badge is the trust signal, checkable on-chain.

04

Gaming & communities

Gate whitelists, tournaments, or mod roles to verified humans — cutting down multi-accounting and bot abuse without collecting IDs.

Pipeline

How it works

Three steps, fully automated, end to end.

1

Face detection & encoding

Detects the face in your photo and encodes it into a 128-dimension vector, then hashes it (SHA-256) — the raw biometric never leaves your machine.

face_recognitiondlibSHA-256
2

Real web / social search

Runs a genuine reverse-image search (Google Lens via SerpApi) against the live web to find an actual matching post — not a hardcoded result.

Google LensSerpApi
3

Soulbound badge minted on-chain

Hashes the matched post and mints a non-transferable NFT to your wallet on Sepolia, then re-reads it on-chain to prove the record hasn't been tampered with.

Solidityweb3.pySepolia
